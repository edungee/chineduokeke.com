Premature scaling is usually framed as an engineering mistake. We argue it is just as often a product one: features, markets and complexity added before the core product has earned them.

We draw on our own experience building a fintech product, from running on a single EC2 instance to expanding the product too early and shutting down a feature that wasn't ready.

## What we cover

- **How to tell foundational investment from unnecessary complexity.**
- **Why startups need room to pivot**, and how early scaling work quietly takes that room away.
- **When preparing for scale early is actually the right call.**

## Where we land

Scale is a problem you earn. Solve the core problem well first, then build for the load that success brings.
