"Technical debt is a product decision." We take that statement and pull it apart from both sides.

Chinedu starts from the engineering view: refactoring, database migrations and cleanup work. Bosun focuses on the product choices underneath it, meaning what gets built now, what gets deferred, and why teams so rarely go back for the deferred work.

## What we cover

- **When paying down debt should take priority over new features.**
- **How performance, reliability and customer impact** should shape that call.
- **Why engineers need a business case** for work users will never see.
- **How AI coding agents** can clear old debt quickly while creating new problems of their own.

## The question we keep coming back to

What will it cost if this debt isn't paid? Framing debt that way turns it from an engineering complaint into a decision the whole team can weigh.
