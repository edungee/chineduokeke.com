Most early-stage startups don't have a product manager, and many do fine without one. So is the role a luxury that comes with scale, or something a young company is quietly missing?

Bosun comes at it from his experience building product at an early-stage startup. Chinedu pushes for a wider definition of product management, one that is about more than tooling, rituals and process.

## What we cover

- **Why founders are usually the first product managers**, whether or not they call it that.
- **Owning product decisions vs. running product operations.** These are different jobs, and confusing them is how teams end up hiring the wrong person.
- **When a startup should hire someone full-time for product**, and what should be true before it does.
- **How AI might change the role.**

## Where we land

Product management is about delivering value to customers and to the business. Someone has to own that from day one. The open question is when it needs to be a dedicated person rather than one of the founder's many hats.
