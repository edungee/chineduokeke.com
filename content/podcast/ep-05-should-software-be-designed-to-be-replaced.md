Should replaceability be a design goal from the start? Bosun argues for it; Chinedu questions how much a startup should invest before it knows what customers need.

We draw on our experience building a fintech product together, including what happened when we introduced a second version of the application.

## What we cover

- **Vendor lock-in and tightly coupled systems**, and how they affect the cost of moving to something new.
- **Legacy code that teams depend on but struggle to understand.**
- **The tension between shipping quickly and leaving room for change**, especially before product-market fit.
- **When designing for replacement becomes premature optimisation.**
- **Who should decide when software needs replacing:** product, engineering or the business.

## The question we keep coming back to

How much future flexibility is worth paying for today? We explore when a rewrite is justified and when keeping a working system is the better choice.
