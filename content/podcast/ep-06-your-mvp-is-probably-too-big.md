Building faster does not tell you which assumptions are worth testing. We discuss how to narrow an MVP around the question that matters most.

## What we cover

- Testing demand and willingness to pay before expanding the feature list.
- The limits of waitlists and market research.
- How AI changes development costs without removing the need to learn.
- Competing with established products, using Linear and Jira as an example.
- Keeping early versions focused through manual processes and simple architecture.
