Fast decisions are one benefit of a small team. We explore whether that speed translates into better products, and what happens when critical knowledge rests with a few people.

## What we cover

- Balancing speed, shared context and delivery capacity.
- How business stage changes the trade-offs of team size.
- Knowledge loss when key engineers leave.
- Using AI gains to increase output or reduce costs.
- Developing future senior engineers and setting realistic delivery expectations.
