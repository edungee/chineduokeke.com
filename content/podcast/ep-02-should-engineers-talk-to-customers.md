Should engineers hear from customers first-hand, or should the product team sit between them and translate?

We both start the episode saying engineers should have direct customer exposure, then quickly discover we mean very different things by it.

## What we cover

- **What gets lost** when customer problems are filtered through a product team before they reach engineering.
- **Why customer context improves engineering judgement**, especially on trade-offs nobody writes into a ticket.
- **The danger of treating customer requests as specifications.**
- **The real cost** of pulling engineers out of focus time and into more meetings.

## Where we land

A practical middle ground. Engineers should sometimes hear directly from customers and ask questions through an engineering lens. The goal of those calls is to understand the problem, not to design the solution live.
